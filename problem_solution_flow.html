System Architecture & Flow

Civic Problem-Solution Workflow

Complete Lifecycle: Citizen Report → Government Review → University Assignment → Public Solution

SIH 2026 Governance Model
Stage 1 01

Citizen Issue Report

Citizens report local civic issues (roads, water, waste) with GPS map location & photo proof.

Actor: Citizens & Community
Output: Geotagged Issue Entry
Stage 2 02

Government Moderation

Gov admin reviews reports, approves genuine entries, and routes them to matched universities based on district & domain.

Actor: Government Admin
Output: Approved & Assigned Issue
Stage 3 03

University Login & Decision

University logs into /university/login, accepts or rejects request with rationale, transmitting decision back to Government.

Actor: University Portal
Output: Accept/Reject Feed to Gov
Stage 4 04

Faculty & Student Assignment

University assigns an official Faculty Mentor and Student Team members to lead R&D and solution execution.

Actor: Academic Dept / Institution
Output: Assigned Project Team
Stage 5 05

Public Report & Solution

University submits formal project reports & solution proposals, stored publicly for all citizens & industry partners to view.

Actor: University R&D Team
Output: Public Project Report
Stage 6 06

Gov Oversight & Impact

Government dashboard tracks real-time acceptances/rejections, project stages, IP/startup outcomes, and community impact.

Actor: Government & Industry
Output: Deployment & Impact Metrics

Data Flow & Signal Matrix

Action Step Initiator Recipient / Feed Data Transmitted
1. Institutional Login University Administrator `/university/login` Credentials → Direct Dashboard Redirect
2. Request Accept / Reject University Portal Government Admin & Dashboard Status (Accepted/Rejected) + Official Rationale
3. Faculty & Student Assignment University Portal Database (`project_teams`) Faculty Mentor Email + Assigned Student Emails
4. Project Report Submission University R&D Team Public Portal (Visible to All) Title, Executive Summary, Deliverables, Outcomes
